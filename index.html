About Shakeef Physics

Bridging Deep Physics Intuition with Modern Technology

Founded by Uthumalebbe Mohammed Shakeef, Shakeef Physics was built with a single mission: to eliminate the fear of A/L Physics among Sri Lankan students.

By combining disciplined classroom pedagogy with software-driven learning tools—such as interactive simulations, structured digital tutes, and AI-powered doubt resolution—students learn why a formula works, not just how to memorize it.

  • Complete Local Syllabus (English & Tamil Medium Support)
  • Weekly Timed MCQ & Structured Essay Evaluations
  • Dedicated LMS with MySQL Progress & Attendance Tracking

Our Vision

To make high-quality, technology-enabled Physics education accessible to every student across the island, empowering them to achieve top district and island ranks.
